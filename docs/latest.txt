Your injury has a date. Your brain's capacity to change does not.

Research shows neuroplasticity — the brain's ability to form new connections — remains active long after SCI, not just in the first months.

Studies link psychological resilience directly to greater participation in rehabilitation and stronger functional outcomes over time.

You are not stuck. The science says so.

If you're navigating the mental weight of SCI, the Reeve Foundation Helpline is free: 1-800-539-7309.
