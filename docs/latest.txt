Recovery after spinal cord injury is not just physical — your mindset matters deeply. 💙

Research consistently shows that people with SCI who maintain a sense of hope and purpose tend to engage more actively in rehabilitation and report higher quality of life. That's not toxic positivity. That's science.

Some days are incredibly hard. Grief, frustration, and fear are all valid. And within that, finding even one small reason to show up today can make a real difference — for your body and your mind.

✅ Hope fuels effort — not the other way around.
✅ Small daily wins build momentum over time.
✅ You don't have to carry this alone.

If you or someone you love is navigating the emotional weight of SCI, free peer support is available through the Christopher & Dana Reeve Foundation Paralysis Resource Center: 1-800-539-7309.
