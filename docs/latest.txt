A wireless brain-spine interface let a man with complete thoracic SCI walk again — not via robotics, but his own muscles.

Researchers at EPFL implanted a brain decoder + spinal stimulator that translate motor-cortex signals into targeted leg movements with ~100ms latency.

The participant walked over 100 meters continuously and retained improved function even when the device was off — suggesting neuroplastic rewiring.

This is a proof-of-concept in N=1. Larger trials are underway.
