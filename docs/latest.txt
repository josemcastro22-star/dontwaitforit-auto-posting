Recovery after a spinal cord injury isn't only physical — your mindset matters deeply. 💙

Research consistently shows that people who maintain a sense of hope and purpose tend to engage more fully in rehabilitation and report higher quality of life after SCI. That's not toxic positivity. That's science.

It doesn't mean every day has to feel hopeful. Hard days are real. Grief is valid. But even on the hardest days, small moments of connection, movement, or meaning can quietly build resilience over time.

If you're struggling emotionally after an injury — yours or a loved one's — you're not alone, and support is available.
