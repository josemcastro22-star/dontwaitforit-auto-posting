Recovery after a spinal cord injury isn't only physical — your mindset matters deeply. 💙

Research consistently shows that people with SCI who maintain a sense of hope and purpose tend to engage more fully in rehabilitation and report higher quality of life. That's not toxic positivity — it's science.

Hard days are real. Grief is real. And so is the capacity to adapt, connect, and find meaning on this journey.

If you're struggling emotionally after SCI, you're not alone — and support is available.
