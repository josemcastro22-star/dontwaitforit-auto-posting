Shoulder injuries affect up to 73% of manual wheelchair users over time — new pushrim sensor tech is changing that math.

SmartWheel and next-gen successors embed force/torque sensors directly into pushrims, streaming biomechanical data to a paired app in real time. Clinicians and users can identify high-risk stroke mechanics before cumulative damage sets in. Studies show biofeedback-guided training reduces peak shoulder load by roughly 40% within 8 weeks.

This is preventive engineering, not a workaround.
