Recovery isn't only physical — it starts between your ears. 🧠💙

Research consistently shows that people living with spinal cord injuries who cultivate a hopeful, flexible mindset tend to engage more fully in rehabilitation and report higher quality of life. That's not toxic positivity — that's evidence.

Some days are genuinely hard. Grief, frustration, and fear are real parts of this journey. But so is the capacity to adapt, find meaning, and keep moving forward — even slowly.

If you're struggling today, that's okay. Reach out to someone who gets it.

You are not your injury. You are still becoming. 🌱
