Flat tires are the #1 reported mechanical failure for manual wheelchair users — Loopwheels and newer entrants are pushing airless tech forward, but 2024 saw a notable step: Greentyre's airless foam-fill wheelchair tire line expanded to North American distributors, offering puncture immunity with reduced rolling resistance.

An estimated 3.3 million wheelchair users in the U.S. rely on manual chairs daily. Tire failure causes falls, missed appointments, and lost independence — not a minor inconvenience.

Airless designs remove the pressure-maintenance burden entirely, critical for users with limited hand function.
