Spinal cord injuries don't discriminate — and the numbers make that clear.

Approximately 18,000 Americans sustain a new SCI each year. That's roughly 49 people every single day.

An estimated 302,000 people in the U.S. are currently living with SCI. Falls and vehicle crashes remain the two leading causes.

Awareness drives funding. Funding drives research.
