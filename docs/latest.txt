Every year, roughly 17,000 people in the U.S. receive a life-changing spinal cord injury diagnosis. Behind every statistic is a person — a family — navigating a new reality.

Awareness is the first step toward better funding, better research, and better outcomes for the SCI community. 💙

📌 Key facts from the National SCI Statistical Center (2023):
→ Motor vehicle crashes are the #1 cause
→ The average age at injury has risen to ~43
→ Nearly 8 in 10 new injuries occur in men

Approximately 302,000 people are currently living with SCI in the United States. That community deserves continued investment in rehabilitation, neuroprosthetics, and recovery research.
