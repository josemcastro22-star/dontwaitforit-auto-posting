Your nervous system is not static — and neither is your mind.

Research shows neuroplasticity continues long after SCI, meaning the brain keeps forming new connections. Studies also link a strong sense of personal agency to measurably lower rates of depression and anxiety in people living with paralysis. That connection between mindset and neurobiology is real, not motivational fluff.

If you're navigating the mental side of SCI, the Reeve Foundation Peer & Family Support Program connects you with trained peers who've been there: 1-800-539-7309.
