Recovery after a spinal cord injury isn't only physical — your mindset matters deeply. 💙

Research consistently shows that people with a positive but realistic outlook tend to engage more fully in rehabilitation, build stronger support networks, and report higher quality of life after SCI.

That doesn't mean forcing positivity on hard days. It means giving yourself permission to find meaning, lean on others, and celebrate the small wins that add up over time.

If you or someone you love is navigating the emotional weight of SCI, you don't have to do it alone.
