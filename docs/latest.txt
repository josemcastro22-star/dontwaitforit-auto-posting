Your nervous system is not frozen in time — and neither are you.

Research confirms neuroplasticity continues long after SCI, meaning the brain keeps forming new connections. Studies show that psychological resilience and a growth-oriented mindset are independently associated with higher quality of life scores in people living with paralysis. One 2021 review found that positive coping strategies correlated with reduced secondary health complications in SCI populations.

You are not defined by the day of injury. The brain is still listening.
