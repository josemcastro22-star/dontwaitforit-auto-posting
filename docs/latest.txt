A digital bridge between brain and spinal cord restored intentional walking in a man with complete thoracic paralysis.

Researchers at EPFL implanted epidural electrodes targeting lumbar locomotor regions, paired with a cortical recording implant. The system decoded intended movement and delivered targeted stimulation within milliseconds — no wired tether, no external robotics.

The participant walked overground, climbed stairs, and navigated ramps. Neurological recovery also persisted even when the device was off.

This is a peer-reviewed result published in Nature, 2023.
