Spinal cord injury is not rare — it's an ongoing public health reality.

Approximately 18,000 Americans sustain a new SCI each year. Around 302,000 people are currently living with SCI in the U.S.

Motor vehicle crashes remain the leading cause at 38.6%, followed by falls at 31.8%. The average age at injury has shifted to 43 — older than in previous decades.

Understanding the scale drives better funding, research, and policy.
