Your nervous system is not frozen in time.

Research confirms the brain continues forming new connections long after a spinal cord injury — a process called neuroplasticity. How you think, cope, and engage with recovery directly influences this process.

Chronic psychological stress elevates cortisol, which studies link to reduced synaptic plasticity. Protective mindsets — including perceived control and social support — are associated with better functional outcomes post-SCI.

You are not just waiting. You are actively part of the process.
