What if the spinal cord isn't as silent as we thought? 🔬

Groundbreaking research using epidural electrical stimulation (EES) showed that people living with complete paralysis could voluntarily move their legs again — years after their injury.

This doesn't mean a cure. But it does mean the nervous system retains more potential than once believed — and scientists are working hard to understand why.

Here's what the research found:
✅ Targeted electrical pulses can reactivate spinal circuits
✅ Voluntary movement returned in participants with long-standing injuries
✅ Larger trials are now underway to test safety and scalability

Every study like this brings the SCI community one step closer to better options. 💙
