Recovery after a spinal cord injury isn't only physical — your mindset matters deeply.

Research shows that people with SCI who cultivate hope, self-compassion, and social connection tend to report better quality of life and stronger engagement in rehabilitation.

That doesn't mean you have to feel positive every day. Hard days are real. Grief is valid. But gently building mental resilience — one small step at a time — is part of the journey too.

If you're navigating the emotional weight of SCI, you don't have to do it alone. 💙

📞 Need support? The Craig H. Neff Center and NAMI Helpline (1-800-950-6264) offer resources for people living with SCI and their families.
