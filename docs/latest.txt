Your mental state is not separate from your recovery — it's part of it.

Research consistently shows that psychological resilience and self-efficacy are strong predictors of functional outcomes after spinal cord injury.

People with SCI who report higher perceived control engage more consistently in rehabilitation and report better quality of life scores over time.

Peer-to-peer connection is one of the most evidence-backed tools available — and it costs nothing to start.

If you're navigating the mental weight of SCI, the Christopher & Dana Reeve Foundation Paralysis Resource Center offers free peer mentoring: 1-800-539-7309.
